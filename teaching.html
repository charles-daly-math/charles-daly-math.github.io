Teaching

Teaching is a huge part of being in the mathematical community. Either in a large lecture or a small seminar, we're always trying to share mathematics in some capacity. It's one of the many reasons I love mathematics so much! Here you'll find some syllabi and information about classes I taught in the past. Feel free to reach out if you want access to some resources I've made. Unless otherwise stated I was course chair for the classes below.
